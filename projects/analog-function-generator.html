Analog Function Generator and PCB

A multi-stage op-amp function generator, taken from simulation to a hand-soldered board and measured against the model on an oscilloscope.

My roleSimulation, schematic, layout, assembly, test
  • Micro-Cap
  • Altium Designer
  • Op-amps
  • Oscilloscope

DesignTopology

  • A TL082 oscillator core produces the square wave.
  • An integrator stage turns it into a triangle.
  • A shaping and amplification stage produces the sine.

Micro-CapSimulate first

I tuned the circuit in Micro-Cap 12 to 4.4 kHz at 10 V before ordering any boards. Simulation is cheap; a board revision is not.

HardwareBoard and bring-up

The PCB was routed in Altium Designer, populated with through-hole parts and hand-soldered. I captured each output on an oscilloscope and compared it against the simulated traces. The measured hardware matched the simulation on all three outputs at the design frequency and amplitude.
